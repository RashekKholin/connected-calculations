About significant figures

Trailing zeros matter: 2.00 has three significant figures. Use scientific notation for clarity and mark counted values as exact. Results are rounded for display; calculations keep full precision.

Chemical formulas

Molar mass feeds into the network automatically. Supports parentheses, brackets, subscripts, hydrates, and charges such as SO4^2- (use a standard minus sign). Formula alone does not determine concentration or the van ’t Hoff factor. Atomic weights: CIAAW 2024. Isotope-specific formulas and reaction balancing are not supported.

Equations & assumptions

Gas and osmotic calculations use R = 0.082057366 L·atm·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹. Arrhenius uses R = 8.314462618 J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹. First-order decay, ideal gases, dilute solutions, constant van ’t Hoff factors, and fixed amount for the combined gas law are assumed. Zero or degenerate cases may remain undetermined. The engine solves supplied rearrangements; it does not perform arbitrary simultaneous symbolic algebra. Formula constants and unit conversions are treated as exact. Log/exp precision follows textbook mantissa rules; ln/exp use the same convention. Precision follows the first derivation found and is not uncertainty analysis.
